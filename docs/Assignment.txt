Why This Assignment
Random Forests and boosting are powerful ensemble methods, but achieving a reliable result depends
on much more than fitting a model. Real datasets introduce missing or invalid values, categorical
features, class imbalance, and the risk of data leakage. A good practitioner must select appropriate
methods, compare them fairly, and explain the resulting predictions.
This assignment evaluates your ability to:
• Prepare realistic tabular data without leaking information from the test set.
• Explain the difference between bagging and boosting through experiments.
• Evaluate models beyond accuracy, especially when classes are imbalanced.
The emphasis is on sound decisions and clear interpretation, not on achieving the highest possible
accuracy.
